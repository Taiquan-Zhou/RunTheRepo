## Gate conclusion and meaning of 2048

Authoritative ruling: `RG1_CAPACITY_GATE=FAIL no_common_bounded_total_through_8192`.

This does not mean the WSL host disk is full; the host had over 100 GiB free.
`2048 MiB` is the per-sandbox total disk budget, divided into Docker-data,
workspace, and root allocations. n8n exhausted Docker-data at 4096 MiB; at
8192 MiB it timed out without a completed report. There is therefore no evidence
that changing the default to 8192 would solve the problem.

Consequences: keep the current 2048 MiB default; do not create
`2026-09-02-m7.5-rg1-policy.md`; do not run a policy canary or claim release
readiness. Any timeout/observability investigation or production correction
needs a new bounded design, independent review, and Owner approval.
